Flora Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Who we are

Flora is being developed as part of Floko, an independent project providing accessibility, sensory and journey information.

The person responsible for handling personal information collected through Flora's registration forms is:

Data controller: Stella Waterhouse, operating Floko as an independent project.

Privacy contact: stella.waterhouse@gmail.com

You can also contact us using the privacy request form below.

Information we collect

When you register your interest through Flora's website, we collect:

  • Your email address.
  • Whether you registered as a traveller, venue or prospective collaborator.
  • For Future Flora registrations, your selected area of interest.
  • Your agreement to receive relevant communications.

If you make a privacy request, we also collect the type of request and any information you choose to provide.

Formspree may process technical information associated with form submissions.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Contact you about Flora's development and availability.
  • Send updates relevant to your registration.
  • Respond to enquiries about participation or collaboration.
  • Manage registration lists and communication preferences.
  • Respond to privacy requests and meet relevant legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information or use your registration to send unrelated marketing.

Our legal basis

We rely on your consent to send the communications you have requested. You may withdraw that consent at any time.

We may also need to process privacy requests to comply with our legal obligations under applicable data protection law.

Who processes your information

Our registration and privacy request forms are provided by Formspree, which receives and processes form submissions on our behalf.

Formspree states that it uses Standard Contractual Clauses as safeguards for international data transfers. Information submitted through Formspree may be processed outside the UK.

You can read more about Formspree's published security and privacy arrangements at Formspree Security and Privacy .

Access to registration submissions is limited to people authorised to manage Flora's registrations and privacy requests.

How long we keep your information

We review registration information at least once every 12 months.

Registration information will normally be deleted within 12 months of your last meaningful interaction with Flora, unless there is a lawful reason to retain it for longer.

If you withdraw consent, we will stop sending the relevant communications and delete your registration details unless we need to retain limited information for a lawful reason.

Privacy requests and related correspondence will be kept only as long as reasonably necessary to deal with the request and demonstrate compliance with applicable law.

Your privacy rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information held about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information.
  • Restrict certain uses of your information.
  • Object to certain processing.
  • Request your information in a portable format.
  • Withdraw your consent.

You can make a request by emailing us or using the form below. We may need to verify your identity before disclosing or changing personal information.

You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) .

Your choices

Registration is voluntary. You do not need to register simply to read information on Flora's website.

Manage your privacy preferences

Use this form to unsubscribe, request access to or deletion of your information, correct your details, or ask another privacy question.

Submitting this form sends your request through Formspree for review. It does not automatically unsubscribe or delete your information. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable data protection law.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as Flora develops. The latest version will be available on our website.

This notice currently covers Flora's website registration and privacy request forms. Other Flora services may require additional privacy information.
