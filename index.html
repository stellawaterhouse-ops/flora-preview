Meet Flora

Know what to expect, from the start of your journey to your destination.

Flora is being developed to bring together practical accessibility, sensory and journey information, helping people understand what they may encounter along the way and at the places they want to visit.

Working with venues and trusted information sources, Flora aims to provide clear, verified information wherever possible, identify what hasn't yet been verified, and highlight anything that remains unknown.

The aim is simple: to give people enough reliable information to make their own decisions about where they go and what they do.

Why Flora?

Finding somewhere you'd love to visit is one thing. Finding out whether it will actually work for you can be quite another.

Accessibility is about more than whether a facility exists. It matters whether you can reach it, use it, and whether there are conditions attached.

Flora aims to make that information easier to find, understand and trust, while being clear about anything that remains unknown.

Imagine you're planning a visit...

You've found a restaurant you'd like to try. But is the entrance step-free? How noisy is the dining area? Can you reach the accessible toilet without going back outside?

Perhaps you need somewhere to sit along the journey, or want to know whether assistance must be arranged in advance.

These are the sorts of practical questions Flora is being developed to help answer, without guessing when reliable information isn't available.

How Flora will help

Start with where you want to go or what you'd like to do.

1. Tell Flora your plans

Begin with a place, destination or experience you're interested in.

2. Explain what matters

Ask about access, noise, facilities, journeys or your individual requirements.

3. Decide for yourself

Understand what is known, how reliable the information is and what's still missing.

Flora is currently in development. We're building and testing these capabilities as the platform grows.

Future Flora

Help us bring Flora to more places.

Our ambition is to grow Flora through a network of people and organisations who share our belief that reliable visitor information should be easier to find and use.

Become part of Flora's local network

We're interested in hearing from people who would like to work flexibly from home, connecting with venues in their own areas.

The aim is to develop opportunities for local interviewers who can introduce Flora, encourage venues to participate and help gather practical visitor information using a consistent approach.

Collaborate with Flora

We're keen to explore working with people and organisations already active in related fields, including:

  • 3D and virtual tour providers.
  • Accessibility and sensory specialists.
  • Destination Management Companies (DMCs).
  • Local destination and travel experts.
  • Accessible travel and visitor support services.

Rather than duplicating valuable work, we'd like to explore how complementary information and expertise can work together.

Stronger together

Flora is intended to grow through collaboration, helping more people access the information they need to make their own choices.

These are future plans and opportunities we're exploring, rather than established roles or formal partnerships.
